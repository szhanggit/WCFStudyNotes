The client app doesn't accept self signed cert. any idea? (Windows 10 & VS2017)

Selsigned Certificate
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gmxoC7HkylE&list=PL6n9fhu94yhXQS_p1i-HLIftB9Y7Vnxlo&index=102&t=0s
Enable Basic Authentication
https://www.server-world.info/en/note?os=Windows_Server_2019&p=iis&f=8



1. 	Go to the root of IIS. => Double click 'Server Certificates' => Click 'Create Self-Signed Certificate'
2.  Select a specific site. Double click 'SSL Settings' => Check 'Require SSL', click 'Accept', and click Appply.
3.	Double click 'Authentication' => Choose 'Basic Authentication'. => Enable it.
4.	In the web.config on server side, 

	<bindings>
	  <wsHttpBinding>
		<binding name="wsHttp">
		  <security mode="Transport">
			<transport clientCredentialType="Basic"></transport>
		  </security>
		</binding>
	  </wsHttpBinding>
	</bindings>  
	<behaviors>
      <serviceBehaviors>
        <behavior name="mexBehavior">
          <serviceMetadata httpsGetEnabled="true" />
        </behavior>
      </serviceBehaviors>
    </behaviors>
